Welcome! My name is Elena Stella, and I am a Ph.D. candidate in Managerial Economics and Strategy at the Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University.

My research lies at the intersection of corporate finance, political economy, and public economics. I study how firms respond to institutional oversight in environments where monitoring resources are scarce and markets and institutions are vulnerable to corruption, crime, and financial misconduct. My work examines how transparency requirements, enforcement mechanisms, and political access influence firms' strategic decisions and market outcomes. I combine quasi-experimental research designs with novel datasets to capture forms of misconduct and institutional responses that are typically difficult to observe.

**Research interests:** Corporate Finance, Political Economy, Public Economics, Corporate Governance, Law and Finance.
